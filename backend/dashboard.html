Risk Profile

STANDARD
STANDARD — 2% Uses 2% of current account equity as the maximum planned risk for each trade.
AGGRESSIVE — 3.5% Uses 3.5% of current account equity as the maximum planned risk for each trade.
Automatic lot sizing: the website sends the selected risk percentage to the EA bridge. The MT5 EA must calculate the final volume using the actual account equity, symbol contract specifications, tick value, tick size and stop-loss distance. The website does not blindly force 0.01 lots.
